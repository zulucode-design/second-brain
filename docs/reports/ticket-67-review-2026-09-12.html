Diagnosis and remediation

The retry worker refreshed pending notes through note_changed, which sent a new wake signal after every failed embedding attempt. A red regression test reproduced an early retry before the configured interval. The worker now refreshes durable pending rows without waking itself, enters an absolute cooldown after an unavailable result, and coalesces all external wakes during that cooldown. This also prevents vault reconciliation from producing one offline request per note.

The first outage message is logged once. Further failures remain quiet until a valid embedding confirms recovery and resets the outage state.
