Ticket #59 — preserve user-authored note bodies

Reviewed 2026-09-10 against origin/main...fix/59-unexpected-headings, with origin/main fixed at eb05716fef3bc9a002c571a37fc55057e502a9b8.

Cause and correction

The frontmatter serializer called ensure_title_heading for every non-empty save. It inserted the note title as an H1. Renaming and saving again could therefore leave the old inserted heading in the body and add another one for the new title.

The serializer now writes frontmatter followed by the body it was given. The change applies to both fresh serialization and frontmatter merging. Existing headings are not removed: stored Markdown does not reveal whether an older heading came from the app or the user.
